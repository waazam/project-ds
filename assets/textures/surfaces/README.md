# Surface textures (the ski lodge and the church)

Photo surfaces for Act 23's lodge and Act 21's church, sampled by the texture factories through
`SurfaceKit` (`scripts/world/SurfaceKit.cs`). Some are taken whole and tinted; others only lay their grain
under a drawn pattern. They are 256 px, softened and shallow, for the PS2 look.

Made by `tools/Textures/make_surfaces.py` (which downloads the 1k sources to `build/polyhaven/`) from these
Poly Haven textures (https://polyhaven.com), all **CC0** (public domain; credited here with thanks):
