# 2026-09-23c — Act 12 polish: the lake, the row, and the thing under it

Commit: this pass's single commit on `main` (see `git log` for the hash).

A full rebuild of Act 12 against the design brief (`Project_DS_Act 12.docx`): wake at a sunrise
lake, board a rowboat, row across with A and D, a tentacled thing covered in bloodshot eyes breaks
the surface midway, fight the current it leaves, step ashore at the rescue station. The first
version of this act worked but played as a flat brown sheet, a boat that teleported 3.4 m per key
press, and a breach that was an opaque red rectangle over the whole screen. This pass keeps its
story hooks (checkpoints, the Continue marker, the station doorway into Act 13) and rebuilds the rest.

## Story Beats
- **Boarding** is one short animation: along the dock to its edge, a step down into the boat (it
  dips and rocks under the weight), sitting down (the eye drops to a seated height), the mooring line
  cast off, the oars shipped out, a push away from the dock.
- **The calm row**: a slow, unhurried pull through golden mist, birdsong, a loon far out on the
  water. Nine metres before the midpoint the birds stop; a vast shadow slides under the boat;
  something thuds deep below and the boat lurches.
- **The breach** (the act's centrepiece, about thirteen seconds, the view steered throughout):
  1. The lake holds its breath: the boat drifts to a stop, a heartbeat, the view drawn down over the side.
  2. An eye the size of a house opens under the water right beside the boat — pupil blown wide, then
     narrowing to a goat's slit as it finds the player. Whispering.
  3. It shuts; the water domes up; a foghorn bellow from under the lake.
  4. A colossal limb, twice the size of the rest, erupts first and slowest, the view dragged up it as
     it towers; then the ring of others, each in a plume of spray, a ring wave rolling out under the
     boat, the world regraded blood-red for a moment (once, easing away), the field of view punched wide.
  5. Two more rise right beside the boat.
  6. Every eye on every limb — about a hundred — opens at once with a wet chorus and turns to the
     player; a beat; they all blink, together.
  7. The nearest limb arches over the boat and hangs there, dripping, looking down into it.
  8. Another rears and slams into the water alongside; the boat is thrown.
  9. Most of it slides back under, the colossus last; two limbs stay up, watching.
- **The fight back**: the water stays rough, a current runs back toward the watchers and swells shove
  the bow. Roughly two alternating strokes a second holds ground; three makes steady headway.
- **Landing**: the bow grinds up the gravel, the watchers sink, the player ships the oars, stands,
  walks forward and steps over the bow onto the beach, then walks up to the station themselves.

## Level Design
- The lake is now a real lake in a wooded bowl: an irregular oval ~190 m wide and ~96 m long (the
  side shores sit far out in the morning mist — it no longer reads as a pond you could walk round),
  a heightfield shore of mud, gravel beaches and grassy banks rising into hills, a dense fir
  treeline ringing it (the forest's own meshes), birches and snags at the edge, dead trees leaning
  out over the water, reeds, rocks, a fallen log, and lily pads that ride the swell.
- A real sunrise while at the lake: the sun low over the far shore (the glitter path runs toward the
  station), a peach horizon under a blue-violet zenith, gold-lit clouds, warm sun-scattered fog;
  everything is put back when the player goes inside. The light shafts are switched off here (a sun
  this low lays them flat across the sky as grey slabs).
- The water surface really moves: four long-crested swells, a dome where the creature rises, one big
  ring wave, whitecaps and foam — all mirrored in C# so the boat rides exactly the waves on screen.
  The boat's own footprint is cut out of the water, so the lake never shows inside the hull.
- A plank dock on posts with a ramp up from the beach, a mooring line, and a routed sign at its foot:
  "RESCUE STATION 7 — ACROSS THE LAKE, BY BOAT".
- The rescue station is much larger: a 15 x 9 m round-log hall gone grey, gable to the lake under a
  big "FOREST RESCUE SERVICE / STATION 7" board, a sagging full-width porch (one post snapped) with
  life rings, boarded and broken windows — one with a faint steady lamp somewhere inside — a
  half-stripped roof with rafters showing, a lattice radio mast on slack guy wires, a flagpole with a
  rag of a flag, an upturned rescue boat on sawhorses, and the front door hanging open onto darkness.
- Invisible fences keep the player on the two beaches (hip-high along the dock so the boat's prompt
  still works over them).

## Additions
- `lakeparts/`: `LakeShape` (shared geometry and wave maths), `LakeTerrain`, `LakeDressing`,
  `LakeStructures`, `LakeSunrise`, `Rowboat` (a clinker-built boat, "STATION 7" on the bow, oars
  with a real catch/drive/release/recovery stroke), `LakeFx` (splashes, plumes, ripple rings).
- `RowingPrompt`: two keycaps (from the real bindings; stick arrows on a gamepad), the next oar lit,
  a pressed cap dipping, and an arrow showing whether the boat is making headway or being dragged back.
- `LakeCreature` rebuilt: jointed tentacles that writhe, rise bodily out of the water streaming
  drops, slam, and loom; every eye a real little eyeball (veined white, glowing red iris, slit pupil)
  turning to track the camera; a colossus limb; a synchronized blink.
- Water shader: the deep eye and the shadow under the boat, sunrise reflections, whitecaps, hull cut-out.
- `red_grade.gdshader`: the breach's red is a regrade under the PS2 post, not an overlay.
- `PlayerCameraRig`: `PitchSwim` and `Shake` (used by the boat), alongside the existing `RollSwim`.

## Audio
- New synthesized sounds (bubble-resonance water, no noise beds): oar strokes, oarlock and hull
  creaks, boarding, the keel grounding on gravel, wave slaps, an underwater thud, the breach's
  eruption, tentacle slams, three loon calls, and two loops (water lapping at the hull, rough water).
- The breach reuses the foghorn, squelches, whispers and the heartbeat; the dawn chorus and the
  loons only sing while the player is at the lake, and stop for good once it goes quiet.

## Comfort
- Owner playtest: the post-breach chop "was giving me a headache". Storm waves cut to roughly a
  third of their height and slowed; the view now rides only 30% of the boat's pitch and roll; swells,
  slams and the breach shake far less. The red wash stays a single ease-in / ease-out (Reduce
  Flashing softens it further). Nothing in the act flashes or strobes.

## Bug Fixes
- The lake's mist, spray and drips use a new soft radial dot (`LakeFx.SoftDot`): at lake scale the
  shared Puff texture showed its square edges, as grey slabs in the sky and boxes in the spray.
- The sunrise now finds the level's sun and environment through `ForestAtmosphere` (new `Env` / `Sun`
  getters); looked up by node name from the current scene it silently found nothing.
- The walk onto the dock had a 0.3 m vertical lip the player body can't climb; it has a ramp now.
