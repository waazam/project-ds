# 2026-10-11b — The playtest list

The owner played the game through and came back with a list of things to fix, polish or tweak. They also took up all five
of my own suggestions (A–E) and added three more: the waist when looking down, the Act 24 cave and the crawlspace. All of
it went in first; then everything was tested in one pass.

## Shorter where it dragged
- **The rowboat is 15% quicker to the eye.** Its top speed is 2.4 m/s (was 2.1), and each stroke pushes harder to
  match.
- **The stairwell after the forestry house is a quarter shorter**: 30 turns, down from 40. **Running on the stairs is
  just running now.** The sprint that quietly put you back up the shaft is gone.
- **Red light, green light is 45% shorter.** The hallway's first part is 178 m (was 324) and the second 523 m (was 950).
  **It saves the first time you walk past the shadow man.** Taken in the red, you wake just past him, not back at the
  start. (It's a flag within Act 14's end, as Act 22's halfway save is: the checkpoints are compared by order and a new
  one can't go between two.)
- **The Act 24 cave is about a third shorter.** The one right way through was 49 junctions long; it's now cut to about
  two thirds. Where the way passes back beside itself further on, it's joined straight across, and the stretch in between
  is left hanging off it as one more side branch. It's still a maze with one right way through.
- **The crawlspace is a quarter shorter**: 148 cells, down from 200. The zigzag upstairs is straightened and three of the
  swings downstairs are pulled in. Its two real ends, the four arms, its save and its pipes are where they were.

## Light
- **The trees have dark bark now.** The trunks were a fine-grained grey-brown photo that blurred to flat beige at any
  distance (it was the texture, more than the lighting). They're now Poly Haven's willow bark, with deep vertical
  furrows, a darker brown, the furrows pushed near black and a stronger relief. The furrows are larger, so they read
  further off, and the pale upper trunks are gone.
- **The lantern lights more.** Its glow reaches 16 m (was 14), brighter (2.9, was 2.3), and falls off more gently.
- **The blacklight lights the way in Acts 14–20.** With the flame dead, its glow round you was 0.55 at 6 m, which lit
  nothing past arm's reach. It's now 1.1 at 10 m, a paler violet, and the beam reaches 13 m.
- **The bug behind "your lantern literally doesn't light up anything".** In Acts 14–20 the flame is dead, but the
  lantern in the hand still showed a lit flame and glowing glass while giving no light at all, unless the blacklight was
  on. Now the lantern goes to its blacklight by itself once the flame is dead. If you switch the blacklight off, the dark
  lantern is lowered out of view instead of showing a flame that lights nothing.
- **The sewer pipe after the closet has five lamps** down its length, brighter (it had two dim ones). The pipe keeps a
  little of the world's light, so its brick and water read round you, not a black tube. The red-light hallway's fog is
  thinner too.

## Act 2: the lantern teaches the blacklight
A few seconds after you first light the lantern at the camp, the flame gutters out. "The flame's gone out. B: try its
blacklight." The flame won't relight until the blacklight has been on a while and has shown you what's written round
the camp. Then the wick catches again, and B takes you back to the flame. It happens once, and it's saved.

## The arm and the lantern (from the owner's reference shots)
- **The lantern hangs from a tall wire bail**, the fist up at its top, the lantern well down under it, the arm reaching
  out ahead. Before, the fist sat right down on the cap, clamping it.
- **It swings like a lantern on a long bail**: slower and heavier. It answers to your real starts, stops and turns, and
  no longer jitters at a sprint.
- **The sprint is smooth.** The hand's bob is in step with your feet, as the head's is, on smooth curves. The old bounce
  kinked off the ground twice a stride, faster the faster you went. At a run the arm is held in and lower, swinging
  gently, not bouncing.
- **(A) The flame lights the hand that carries it**: a warm light up the underside of the glove and along the sleeve.
- **(E) The head bob is easier on a sprint.** It was growing to 1.4 times the walk's; now it's at most the walk's, a
  little under it running, eased in.

## Your waist, looking down
The coat's hips are gone. The shirt is **tucked into the jeans**, which have a waistband, and there's **a leather belt
with a buckle** round it. Looking down you see your boots, your jeans, the belt and the shirt above it, as one body.
Before, a shapeless dome sat on the legs.

## Sound
- **New footsteps.** They were cartoonish because of tones: the dirt step's heel was a sine that fell in pitch as it
  struck, the mud's suck a falling tone, and the snow's packing a narrow ringing band, all bright and light over a heel
  with no weight. Every step is now built on one boot coming down, all filtered noise: the heel's thud, the sole's slap
  and the toe rolling down after. The ground's own sound rides on that, darker. This covers dirt, gravel, mud, wet and
  dry leaves, twigs, roots and snow.
- **The oars sound like water.** The old strokes were mostly pitched "bloops" and "plinks". Now: a dull sploosh going
  in, a broad swirling wash drawn through, and a spill and a few soft drips coming out. One quiet gulp is the only
  pitched thing left.
- **The stalker clicks far more**, as it did in the older builds:
  - its silences are 12–26 s on the path walk, 9–20 s later (were 25–60 and 20–45);
  - its episodes are 14–26 s;
  - its bursts are longer, with shorter gaps, and a third of them loud;
  - it's heard from 44 m on the path (was 34), 34 m later (was 26).
- **(D) The music is louder all game.** The bus layout held the Music bus at −24 dB, under the ambience and the
  footsteps, so every cue was a murmur. It's set to −16 dB at start. Act 24's boost is lowered by the same amount, so the
  cave's song stays where it was.

## (B) Brightness, before the first game
The first time you start a new game, a black screen comes up with a figure drawn only just above black and the
brightness slider. Raise it until the figure is only just visible, then press Begin. It's shown once.

## (C) Save points

- The stairwell and the crawlspace are short enough now that their single saves serve.
- The lake crossing is still one stretch (it saves at the far shore).
