## The tablecloths (`SkiLodge.Act23.cs`)
- **The cause of the glitching:** the last round added
  - an invisible catcher mesh the cloth collided with;
  - a sideways drag.

  Jolt's soft bodies don't collide with themselves and jitter against mesh colliders, so the cloth folded
  through itself and shivered on the catcher.
- **The fix:**
  - The catcher and its collision layer are gone.
  - The cloth is drawn toward the player again on one smooth curve (lifted, over, down).
  - The cloth is simulated a little finer, and is lighter and stiffer.
- **What's under each sheet** stays hidden until the linen has swept past it, so nothing cuts up through the
  cloth.
- **Setting down:** each sheet is still only frozen once it has come to rest.

## The crawlspace (`SkiLodge.Crawlspace.cs`)
- **What it is:** a wall cavity, after the Evil Dead 2 run and the reference pictures:
  - rough board walls and old brick;
  - studs and joists close overhead;
  - thin blades of light leaking between the boards;
  - just over two metres high and barely a shoulder wide.
- **Its layout:** a claustrophobic maze of about 200 cells (a few minutes at a crouch, longer if the player
  explores its dead ends):
  - short flights of stairs down between floors;
  - dead-end spurs;
  - stray pipes across at head height to crouch under.
- **As it goes:**
  - dust and flakes trickle from the walls;
  - far-off wendigo howls echo through it.
- **How it's built:**
  - the first and last few cells are real, inside the lodge's walls (from 201's bathroom, and out behind the
    dining hall);
  - the maze between them is built out of sight below the lodge, with the player carried between them at two
    seamless hand-overs (same geometry on both sides, no jump);
  - it doubles as the "loading screen" for the lodge's transformation: the lodge freezes while the player is
    in the walls.
- **A save halfway along.**

## The arm (`entities/WendigoArm.cs`)
- **Four times**, from about halfway, the wendigo's arm bursts through the boards:
  - boards splinter and dust bursts;
  - it reaches and gropes after the player.
- **To get past:** crouch under it. If it catches a standing player, it shoves them back ("Get down.").
- **Each time is worse:**
  - a louder howl;
  - a bloodier arm;
  - frost and blood left round the hole after it withdraws.
- **What it looks like:**
  - a giant's limb starved to rope and bone, with a knotted elbow and cords of tendon;
  - corpse-grey going to frostbitten black at the hand, with a wet sheen and frost crusted on it;
  - an oversized hand with long, twice-knuckled fingers ending in hooked black claws.
- **How it moves:** it's driven by two-bone IK, so it really reaches for where the player is.

## Performance (`SkiLodge.Perf.cs`)
- **Outdoors hidden while indoors:** the woods and the church are hidden while the player is inside the lodge.

## Fixes found by the test runs
- **The envelope's lift:** it built a mirrored rotation, which threw an error every frame and left the
  envelope on the bed.
- **The fallen wardrobe:** it landed across the slit and blocked the way out. It now twists aside.
- **Loudness:** a burst sound, a howl and the door explosion were over the +8 dB limit.
- **The front doorway:** there was no opening in the lodge's front wall behind its doors, so blowing the doors
  out showed solid stone.
  - The wall now has a real doorway, held shut by a collider until the wendigo goes through.
  - The outer leaves are trimmed clear of their lintel (they z-fought where they met).
- **The final look:** it now happens from the porch step, out past the doorway. From inside the wall's depth,
  looking left and right only met the jamb.
- **The dining hall's lobby end:** it showed the hall's outside (stone and dark boards) as a black wall. It's now
  panelled and papered like the rest of the room, with the doors through it.
- **204's smudged note:**
  - When it was hidden in the wardrobe or the closet, it hung behind the door, so opening the door left it
    floating in the air beside it. It's now tucked into the inside face of the door.
  - Opening 204's closet could shove the player into a pocket between its swung-out door, the bed and the
    window, with no way out. 204's bed (the room has no sofa) now stands 1.2 m further west, so that corner is
    open.
- **The arm in the dark:** it now brings a faint cold light in through its hole, so it reads against the cavity
  instead of as a black shape. It's a dim rim of light, not a glare.
