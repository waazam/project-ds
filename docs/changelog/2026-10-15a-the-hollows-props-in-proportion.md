# 2026-10-15a — The Hollow's props in proportion: lamps, tables, chairs, stoves, frames and fixtures turned and eased; the car rebuilt

The owner: "do another pass on all props like lamps on the table etc and make sure they're proportioned and not too
blocky looking. For example the green lamp in the room." This pass covers the Hollow and the early acts' places (the
cabin, the shed, the trailhead and the car, the bunker, the forester station and what lies under it, the stairwell's
portraits). Each prop was measured against a real one's size and rebuilt where it was a box or a single cylinder
standing in for something turned, rounded or moulded: round things are lathed at 8-16 sides (PS2-era: low, not boxy),
edges eased. No story beat, puzzle, pickup or interactable moved; every collider is where it was (the friend's table
grew 9 cm taller; the newel post still settles onto it).

The turned and moulded shapes are a new small kit, `scripts/world/HollowProps.cs` (a lathe in place, a lathe's inside, a
mitred moulding, and the props built from them: the banker's lamp, the hanging hurricane lamp, a bare bulb and its
lampholder, a tin can, a kettle, a kitchen chair).

## The car (`ParkProp.Car.cs`, new; it was `ParkProp.Car()`)

The owner: "make the car look less blocky and more real but keep it PS2 style." It was slices of extruded boxes, a box
roof, box bumpers, slab lamps and opaque black glass. Still the same late-90s estate in faded dark green, 4.6 x 1.74 x
1.43 m, backed into its bay with the tailgate up, now about 5.8 k triangles:

- **The body is one smooth skin**: columns along the car, rows from the sill (or the wheel arch, cut round the tyre) up
  to the shoulder; the flanks rolled in at the shoulder and tucked under at the sill, the nose and tail rounded in plan,
  the nose face leaning back, the bonnet crowned and sloping down to the nose; the tail closed below the load floor,
  with tall lamp panels beside the tailgate's opening.
- **The glasshouse** leans in from the belt to the roof: tinted, see-through glass in three side windows, the raked
  windscreen, B, C and D pillars and the cant rails in paint, A pillars rolled over the screen's edges, a crowned roof
  with its rails on feet, the headliner under it.
- **The cabin**, seen through the glass: the floor, the door cards and armrests, a moulded dash with its binnacle and
  centre stack, the steering wheel on its column (three spokes), two bucket seats with headrests, the rear bench. The
  load space, the duffel and the bottle as before; the camera still lies on the load floor at 0.6.
- **Detail**: door shut lines (front, between the doors, the rear door round its arch) and the bonnet's, door
  handles, mirrors on their arms with their glass, the scuttle and two parked wipers, wrap-round bumpers, headlamps
  recessed in black bezels with reflectors behind the lenses and amber indicators, a grille recess with its slats, the
  tail lamps red over amber over reversing in their frames, both plates, rubbing strips between the arches.
- **Wheels**: 65 cm tyres (r 0.325, were 0.31) with rolled sidewalls, dished hubcaps with their vents, arch liners.
- **Paint**: the green in the material, the vertices darkening the flanks against the sun-faded top and carrying mud up
  the sills and thrown back off the tyres.
- Kept: the two collision boxes, the tailgate's hinge and swing, the load floor, the driver's seat point the fifth
  ending uses. **The fifth ending** (`EndingDirector.cs`): the eye is now at the seat's head height (`EyeHeight` 1.0
  with the feet where they were); at standing height it had been 0.6 m above the roof, looking over the bonnet.

Before and after: `test-output/contact_car_before_after.png`; the car in Act 25's light: `test-output/sheet_car_a25.png`.

Looked at and left: the cabin's stove, cot, shelf, crate and the woodpile (rebuilt in 2026-10-14h); the radio desk and
