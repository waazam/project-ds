# 2026-09-26h — Act 20: the switches, the power surge, candlelight and the green ring

Commit: this entry's commit on `main`. From the owner's "The Real Act 20" document, with their knife-switch references.

## The round room
- **Before the power comes back**, the room is lit by bare bulbs in wire cages on a conduit that
  runs round the drum. Their light breathes slowly (no flicker). The candles are dark.
- **The ring of light round the dais** glows a solid, ominous red. It sits on the dais's sloped foot,
  so it rides up with it, and it casts a glow on the floor. It is drawn by a shader
  (`assets/shaders/dais_ring*.gdshader`).
- **Six old knife switches** hang on the side walls, three on each:
  - each has a bakelite plate, copper jaws, yellow warning stickers, black arms, a crossbar with a
    DANGER / HIGH VOLTAGE sign and a red grip;
  - each lever has three positions (down, middle, up); E moves it one notch;
  - they start scrambled, with none already in the right position;
  - wrong settings make the bulbs sag once with the load.
- **The solution**: facing either wall, the left switch goes down, the middle one to the middle and
  the right one up.
- **The surge** plays out over about 10 seconds. The player is held, and the view turns gently
  toward the dais.
  1. The bulbs swell and the buzz climbs.
  2. The bulbs burst one after another round the room.
  3. The room goes dark but for the red ring.
  4. Every candle in the drum lights itself, round the bottom first and then up.
  5. A green light chases round the ring, its tail longer every lap, until the ring is solid green.
- None of it strobes.
- The dais only rises once the power is back. Standing in the middle before that gives
  "Nothing. The ring stays red."
- The power state is saved (flag `round_room_powered`).
- **New sounds**: `lever_throw`, `power_surge`, `candle_ignite` and `ring_charge`.

## The library journal
- A leather journal lies on two ledgers in a gap on the left-hand shelves. By lamplight it is
  nothing special.
- Under the blacklight, a hand glows on its cover and READ on the shelf above it. From then on it
  can be read.
- It holds the owner's riddle: "the lefts are feeling down, the rights are all right, and if
  that's true the middles are doing just fine". It adds: "Both walls say the same thing."
