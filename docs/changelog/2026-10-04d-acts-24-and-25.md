## Act 24: the snow maze (`scripts/world/snowmaze/`)
- **The way down** (`Act24Maze.cs`): the wendigo's tracks lead off the lodge's porch across the snow to a drift with a black hole in it. Walking in fades to black, and they're in the maze's way in: "So quiet down here." (**Checkpoint 31**, Act24Maze).
- **The maze's plan** (`SnowMazeLayout.cs`):
  - an 11 × 11 grid of junctions 9 m apart, cut into a maze by a random spanning tree, so there is one right way through to the middle;
  - loops added only inside the side branches, so they bring the player round without opening a second way through;
  - dead ends, and the great cavern at the centre.
- **The cave itself** (`SnowMazeCave.cs`, `SurfaceNets.cs`): one carved volume, not tiles.
  - The tunnels, the cavern, alcoves, mounds on the floor, skylight shafts and the trench cuts are added together as a distance field.
  - A surface-nets mesher turns it into one smooth, connected cave: about 215,000 triangles, cut into 18 m chunks, each culled and collided on its own.
  - About 2,400 icicles hang in curtains along the roofs (one draw).
  - Pale daylight comes down the skylights in shafts with dust in them.
- **Built without a stall:** the meshing takes about six seconds. It runs on a background thread while Act 23's ending plays, and the chunks are put into the scene a few milliseconds' worth per frame. The one 0.9 s hitch the first version had is gone. Loading into the act builds it all at once, behind the black.
- **The ice** (`assets/shaders/ice_cave.gdshader`):
  - deep blue in the hollows, paler on the bulges, scalloped like the inside of a shell;
  - a faint cold light from inside it;
  - trodden snow on the floor, banked up the walls' feet;
  - the odd crystal glinting as you move.
  - It's kept dark and satin, not glossy. A first version had high-contrast camouflage blotches and glints everywhere, and glared.
- **The trenches** (`SnowMazeDressing.cs`, `tools/Blender/act24.py`): cut into the cavern's floor and shored with posts and planks. The wood is old and grey; the lodge's polished wood read new and orange.
  - In them: duckboards, sandbags, M1 and Stahlhelm helmets, rifles, weapon crates, soldiers' frozen remains still in their coats.
  - Reaching the cavern is a save (**Checkpoint 32**, Act24Trenches): "Trenches. Down here."
- **The crowbar and the crate:** a crowbar lies somewhere in the trenches. A crate stencilled "FLAME THROWER, PORTABLE" is nailed shut until it's prised open with it. "A flamethrower. It still works." (**Checkpoint 33**, Act24Flamethrower). Far off, it screams: it heard, and comes to look.

## The wendigo's hunt (`scripts/entities/WendigoHunter.cs`)
Two brains, after Alien: Isolation, as the owner's document asks.
- **The hunter** has its own senses and never knows where they are without them.
  - **Hearing**, measured along the tunnels, not through the snow: running carries 20 m, walking 8 m, crouching 2.6 m, standing still nothing. Firing the flamethrower carries 30 m. With its back turned, it hears at half range.
  - **Sight:** a cone ahead of it, out to the dark, with nothing seen through the snow. Crouching and a dark lantern make them harder to see.
  - **Modes:** patrol, investigate, search and chase. Walk and run gaits were added to the wendigo's rig (`tools/Blender/wendigo.py`).
- **The director** always knows where they are and never tells it. It keeps a menace gauge. If they've been safe too long, it nudges the wendigo their way; if it has been on them too long, it sends it off elsewhere.
- **Sized for the tunnels:** drawn at two thirds of its height, stooped, with the tunnels' roofs at 3.2 m. At full height it stood through them, and its eye, up in the snow, saw nothing.
- **Burned, it becomes the prey:** it flees and hides, stalks from behind and ambushes. It can still kill. Four burns finish it (**Checkpoint 34**, Act24Finished): it falls against the maze's wall and burns a way out.

## The flamethrower and its fire (`Flamethrower.cs`, `NapalmStream.cs`, `napalm_flame.gdshader`, `FlamethrowerGauge.cs`)
- **In their hands, in view:** the M2-2's gun on the camera. It has two grips, the long olive barrel, the igniter's cartridge drum, and a sooted nozzle with a little pilot flame at its mouth. The hose runs back over the shoulder to the tanks.
  - It's matte olive drab and dark steel. It had read as gold under its own warm light.
  - It sways and kicks back gently when it fires (the owner's motion comfort).
- **A new fire system**, not the webs' particles:
  - **The stream:** a stream of burning fuel thrown at 21 m/s, 125 globs a second, arcing down under its weight and slowed by the air. Each glob is drawn out along its flight, so the stream reads as one rope of fire that opens into a rolling, billowing tongue at its end.
  - **Splash:** where it strikes the snow or the ice it clings and burns for a couple of seconds, spreading.
  - **Smoke:** burnt-out fire rolls up into thick black smoke, trailing over the stream and climbing off what burns.
  - **Light:** a warm light rides the fire, eased, never flickering on and off.
  - **Colour:** dull red, orange, and a deep yellow at the hottest, never white (no glare); soft where it meets a surface. Nothing in it flashes.
  - **Cost:** all of it is two draws and a handful of ray tests a frame. Its draw bounds follow the fire (fixed bounds at the world's origin had culled it away in the maze).
- **Infinite fuel, but heat:** a gauge fills while it fires. Held about 5.5 s, it locks and vents for seven seconds. New sounds: ignite, a seamless roar, stop, overheat, ready (`tools/AudioGen/Act24Sounds.cs`), and a seamless cave ambience.

## Act 25: the endings (`Act25Ending.cs`, `EndingDirector.cs`)
- **The choice:** pictures taken, deaths and time played go into the save, and the owner's table picks one of five endings. A record off the table falls to the nearest one. `--ending=N` forces one, for tests.
- **Out:** the burn-through, then the trailhead. They come out of a hole in a snowbank (behind them, up the trail), the snow thinning to nothing into the autumn woods. Down the trail they pass an old cabin with its door hanging open, and reach the lot and the car.
- **Endings 1, 2 and 5:** put the camera back in the trunk.
  - 1: black, and the credits.
  - 2: black for five seconds, and far off it howls.
  - 5: the fog comes in, but the car really is there; after the black, the engine turns over.
- **Ending 3:** the wendigo crashes down on the car and comes at them burning. They turn, and the stalker is there.
- **Ending 4:** fog. Where the car should be are the stairs, blood running down them a step at a time, and they climb, faster and faster.
- **After the end:** the story's end save and the credits as before. A Continue finds them by the car.
