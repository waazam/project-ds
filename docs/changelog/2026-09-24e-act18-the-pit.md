# 2026-09-24e — Act 18: the pit (boss fight), and the camera kept all game

Commit: this pass's single commit on `main` (see `git log` for the hash).

Built from `Project_DS_The Real Act 18.docx`, the owner's references and their playtest notes during
the build. The references were:
- a yellow handwheel;
- a teal spotlight beam across dark air;
- stage trusses with lamps;
- galvanized catwalk grating;
- walls of pipes (insulated, foil, black steel, copper, cable bundles, gauges);
- the round shaft in a concrete ceiling.

## Story Beats
- **The drop**: yes and yes at the sewer's hole goes black, then out of a round shaft in a concrete
  ceiling, a long fall, and onto a steel catwalk. That is Act 18's save (checkpoint 17), on landing.
- **The room**: a great square pit.
  - A see-through galvanized catwalk, 2.6 m wide, runs right round the top, railed on the pit side.
  - The walls behind it are pipes from catwalk to ceiling: three depths of runs, elbowed risers,
    sagging cable bundles, gauges and small handwheels.
  - Trusses and stage lamps hang overhead.
  - About five feet below the catwalk is a dark ocean of blood with the thing from the lake in it.
    At first only its limbs poke through; its eye-crusted body surfaces halfway through the fight.
- **The intro**: a look down, a bellow, a limb comes up and slams the catwalk nearby (a harmless
  demonstration), then a teal spotlight clunks on from across the room onto the first valve: "The
  valves. Turn them. Drain it." After a death, a short version plays.
- **The fight** is sixteen yellow handwheels on the pipe walls, ten of which the spotlight picks,
  one at a time.
  - The order is random, never a neighbour next, so it's back and forth round the room.
  - A valve is held E to turn (2.6 s). Progress is kept if you have to let go and run.
  - Each valve drains the blood further, sets off a siren, and hurts and rots it. Its limbs
    convulse, and it screams.
  - **The rot** (the owner's request): with every valve its hide goes from glossy purple-grey to
    mottled grey-green, then sore-covered, then sloughing to pale flesh. By the last valve it is a
    zombified thing, its eyes clouded milky while its irises burn hotter, and it thrashes harder and
    faster, rabid.
  - **The slams**:
    - Fourteen limbs rear up over a spot, a red glow thrown on the grating under it (it builds
      steadily, no blinking), and come down. A hit is death ("It crushed you."), back to Act 18's
      start.
    - They come more often, more at once (2, rising to 5), with less warning as it goes, and their
      reach grows from 2 m to 3.2 m.
    - From about a third of the way in, barrages come: a run of limbs down one after another along
      the catwalk, a wave to time a way through.
    - Fairness rules keep it tricky but fair. Circles never overlap, and there is always an escape
      along the catwalk. While you're dodging one, nothing new lands near you, and a barrage is
      never dropped on top of you. Only the red circle kills.
- **The end**: the last valve empties the pit. You're moved to the rail to watch.
  - All 94 of its eyes burst one after another, from the crown down and out along the limbs, each
    in a spray of blood.
  - It shrieks, every limb thrashes once more and drops, and the body sags.
  - Across the pit, a steel door rises onto a clean, well-lit little room: warm light, a rug, a
    table and lamp, an armchair, a plant, a clock. Walking in is Act 18's end (checkpoint 18).
    Act 19 isn't built yet, so the credits roll there.
- **Length**: about 7 minutes for a player (the owner's target, down from 10). The test bot's
  winning fight takes 3.7 minutes of pure fighting with 91 slams, without looking round or
  hesitating.

## The camera, all game
- The photo camera is no longer taken at the first stairs (Act 2): it stays with the player for the
  whole game. Older saves from past Act 2 get it back on Continue.

## Additions
- `Leviathan`: the boss. It has a body and fourteen curve-posed tentacles, aimable slams, rot, the
  eye bursts and death.
- `rot_skin.gdshader`: the rotting hide.
- `BossRoom` (with `BossRoom.Fight.cs`), and `bossparts/Valve` and `bossparts/BossTextures` (the
  grating, pipe insulation, foil, paint and gauge faces).
- `light_beam.gdshader`: the spotlight's visible teal shaft.
- The valves' handwheels are smooth true tori with round spokes (after the owner's note that the
  first ones looked jagged).
- New checkpoints: `Act18Finished` = 18. `Act17Finished` now respawns on the catwalk (the fight
  starts over). There is a new flag, `act18_intro_seen`.
- `--story-from=18`.

## Audio (after the owner's notes)
- Blood is viscous: slow heavy sloshes and gloops, never bubbles. This covers the pit's ambience and
  the draining.
- Valves are squeaky hinges: short, high stick-slip squeals as you turn, and a clunk into place.
- A menacing mechanical siren wails when a valve is done.
- A limb slamming the catwalk is a heavy wet hit, with the grating ringing and rattling and blood
  thrown off it. A limb rearing up is a slow wet slither of liquid pouring off it.
- There is a new pit ambience: a slow huge heartbeat, a sour low drone, and the blood heaving below.
