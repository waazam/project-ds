# 2026-09-21a — Acts 8-11: the bunker, the maze, and the giant's return

Commits: `bc01e36`, `6ca0122`

The story's back half, taking the player from the burning cabin through the
bunker and out to a final confrontation. The full Acts 1-11 outline was also
written up as a standing design doc this session.

## Story Beats
- **Acts 8-10**: the bunker's vault-door entrance, an arched underground
  hallway whose lights redden the deeper you go, a CRT room whose puzzle
  clears to the stairs, and a maze escape ending in a dropped,
  static-hissing walkie-talkie.
- **Act 11 ("The Third Man")**: the radio's first exchange, a walk back to
  the original stairs (now rebuilt impossibly tall and fogged from view
  partway up), a long silent climb, and Act 4's giant waiting at the top —
  its eyes open before it closes the distance and touches the player.
- **Act 7 intensified**: a more intense cabin fire, "come up and see"
  whispers using recorded voice takes with randomized pitch/distance/
  distortion, a rare "turn around" cue while walking, and the bunker
  appearing after the fire.

## Level Design
- Cabin gable, door, and shed geometry fixes.
- A redesigned, more compact compass.
- A bigger, better-lit Act 6 clearing with a red spotlight payoff.

## Additions
- A stamina meter gating sprint.
- A scroll-cycled equipped-item HUD.
- A master volume slider in both menus.
- `--skip-to-act7` / `--skip-to-act11` dev fast-path flags for `--autotest`.

## Subtractions
- Removed a maze lighting effect that read as a seizure risk rather than
  atmosphere.

## Bug Fixes
- Hardened a couple of pre-existing flaky autotest checks.
